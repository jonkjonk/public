Rensha Loop Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Rensha Loop does not collect, store on a server, or sell personal information. The app has no account and no analytics.

The camera is used only to take the burst on your phone. Those photos stay on the device unless you save or share them. Save adds the film or the frame grid to your photo library. Share opens the system share sheet, and the file goes only to the place you pick.

Frame count, interval, playback speed, film, and grain are saved on the device so the app can remember them. They are not sent off the phone.

Rensha Loop does not track you across apps or websites.
